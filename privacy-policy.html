1. What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

Personal Information Provided by You

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Account and contact details — your name, email address and phone number.
  • Authentication data — the identifier issued by Apple or Google when you sign in. Bhogi does not use passwords, and we never see or store one.
  • Food preferences — the dishes you pick during the food quiz, the dishes you save as bookmarks, and the dishes you mark “not for me.”
  • Allergies, intolerances and dietary restrictions — see Sensitive Information below.
  • Social activity — your friends on Bhogi, friend requests you send or receive, and the dinner invitations you send or receive, including the restaurant, the dish, the time and any note you write.
  • Feedback — ratings and notes you leave after a meal.
  • Waitlist details — if you sign up on our website, the name, ZIP code, email address and optional phone number you enter in the waitlist form.

Sensitive Information

Bhogi lets you record food allergies, intolerances and dietary restrictions so that we can filter out dishes that are unsafe or unsuitable for you. In the EEA, the UK and some other jurisdictions this is treated as health-related data, a “special category” of personal information.

  • It is optional. You can use Bhogi without providing it, and the app offers an explicit “I don’t have any sensitivities” choice.
  • We rely on your consent to process it, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by editing or clearing your sensitivities in the app.
  • We use it for one purpose — filtering and ranking dish recommendations. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with other users.
  • When you view a combined recommendation feed with a friend, our systems exclude dishes that are unsafe for either of you. Your friend sees the resulting dish list — not your underlying allergen or dietary information.

We do not process other sensitive categories, such as your racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic or biometric data, or sexual orientation.

Sign-In Provider Data

You register and sign in to Bhogi using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. When you do, we receive a limited profile from that provider — typically your name, your email address and a unique account identifier. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple’s private relay address and can reach you only through it. We do not receive your contact list, friends list or social graph from either provider. See the section called “How Do We Handle Your Social Logins?” below.

Application Data

The Bhogi mobile app asks for the following device permissions. Each one is optional, each is requested only when you first use the feature that needs it, and you can change or revoke any of them at any time in your device’s settings.

  • Precise location. With your permission, Bhogi uses your device’s location to find restaurants near you, to rank dish recommendations by how far away they are, and to give you walking directions. Your coordinates are sent to our servers each time your recommendations are refreshed so that we can work out which restaurants are nearby. We use location only while the app is in use — we do not track your location in the background, and we do not build a history of where you have been. If you decline, Bhogi falls back to a default city centre and recommendations will be less relevant.
  • Contacts. With your permission, Bhogi reads your device’s address book so you can find people to invite. Your address book is processed on your device and is never uploaded to our servers. Searching and matching happen locally on your phone. The only contact details that ever reach us are those of a specific person you deliberately choose to invite — see “Inviting someone who is not yet on Bhogi”.
  • Push notifications. With your permission, we send notifications about friend requests, dinner invitations and replies to them. To do this we store a device push token issued by Apple or Google. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device’s settings; the token is cleared when you sign out.

Bhogi does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, SMS or call logs, and does not read or send text messages on your behalf. When you invite someone, your own messaging app opens with a prefilled message and you send it — Bhogi never sends a message itself.

Information About Other People That You Give Us

Bhogi lets you invite people who do not yet have an account. When you choose a person from your address book and confirm the invitation, we receive and store that person’s phone number, and their email address if your contact card has one, so that we can create the invitation, generate its link, and connect you as friends automatically if they sign up. We also store your relationship to that invitation so we can show you whether it is still outstanding.

We ask you to invite only people who would reasonably expect to hear from you. If you are the person who was invited and you would like that record removed, email support@bhogi.ai from, or naming, the number in question and we will delete it. We explain exactly what happens to an invitation, including how long we keep it, in “Inviting someone who is not yet on Bhogi”.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information) but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, referring URLs, device name, country, location, information about how and when you use our Services, and other technical information. In Short: We share information with the providers who run Bhogi for us, and with other users when you choose to add a friend, send a dinner invitation, or invite someone to join. We do not sell your information.

Bhogi is a social app, so some of your information is shown to other people by design. This section explains exactly what is shared, with whom, and what you control.

With other Bhogi users

When you use the social features, the following information about you becomes visible to others:

  • When someone searches for you on Bhogi, they can see your name, your initials and your profile picture.
  • Once you are friends with someone, they can additionally see whether you are currently active on Bhogi (an online or offline indicator).
  • When you send or accept a dinner invitation, the other person sees your name and profile picture, the restaurant, the dish you attached if any, the proposed time, and any note you wrote. After the meal, feedback you submit about the invitation is visible to us, not to the other diner.
  • When you view recommendations together with a friend, both of you see the same combined dish list. Your friend does not see your allergies, dietary restrictions, quiz answers, saved dishes or dismissed dishes — only the resulting recommendations.

You control this: you can decline or remove a friend, cancel or decline an invitation, and delete your account at any time.

Inviting someone who is not yet on Bhogi

Because this involves someone else’s personal information, we want to be precise about it.

  • Choosing a contact. Your address book is read on your device only. We do not upload, copy or store your contact list.
  • Creating the invitation. When you confirm an invitation for a specific person, we receive that person’s phone number — and their email address if your contact card includes one — and create an invitation record linked to your account, with a unique invitation link.
  • Sending it. Your device’s own share sheet opens with a prefilled message containing the link, and you send it through whichever app you choose. Bhogi does not send text messages, emails or any other message to the people you invite. If you close the share sheet without sending, nothing is delivered.
  • Reminders. If an invitation is still outstanding, you may ask us to prompt it again from within the app. A limited number of reminders is available per invitation.
  • If they join. We match the invitation to their new account so the two of you can be connected as friends, then the invitation is treated as used.
  • If they never join. We keep the invitation only as long as it is useful for this purpose, and no longer than 12 months, after which the invited person’s phone number and email address are deleted. See “How Long Do We Keep Your Information?”.
  • If you were invited and want the record removed sooner, email support@bhogi.ai and we will delete it. You do not need a Bhogi account to make that request.

We do not use invited people’s contact details for marketing, we do not add them to any mailing list, and we do not sell or otherwise disclose them.

In other situations

  • Business Transfers. We may share or transfer your information in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business to another company.
  • Legal obligations and safety. We may disclose information where we are required to by law, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to investigate or prevent fraud or abuse, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights or safety of any person.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for cross-context behavioural or targeted advertising.
